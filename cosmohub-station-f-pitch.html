COSMOHUB // VISION DECKEARLY-STAGE CONCEPT
The intelligence layer for the space ecosystem

Know what is happening in space.
Know where you fit.

CosmoHub is an early-stage platform concept for connecting fragmented space knowledge, research, organizations, opportunities, projects, and talent through one structured intelligence layer.

NEWS→RESEARCH→KNOWLEDGE GRAPH→OPPORTUNITIES→PEOPLE
COSMOHUB // 01THE GAP
The problem

The space economy is expanding.
Its information layer is fragmented.

Discovery

Knowledge is scattered

Research, mission updates, university activity, grants, jobs, communities, and technical learning live across disconnected systems.

Navigation

Entry paths are unclear

Students and early-career talent can find information, but struggle to understand which skills, projects, institutions, and opportunities connect.

Institutions

Signals are hard to map

Organizations publish valuable information, but it is rarely structured into a connected view of the wider ecosystem.

The problem is not a lack of space information. It is a lack of context, structure, and connection.
COSMOHUB // 02PRODUCT THESIS
The thesis

Build a space knowledge graph, then make it useful.

CosmoHub aims to organize the ecosystem around entities and relationships rather than isolated pages and feeds.

Entities

What exists?

People, universities, companies, labs, missions, technologies, papers, grants, projects, jobs, and learning resources.

Relationships

How are they connected?

Who researches what, which skills map to which missions, where opportunities originate, and which institutions shape each domain.

Intelligence

What should I do next?

Turn the graph into discovery, personalized pathways, research navigation, opportunity matching, and ecosystem intelligence.

COSMOHUB // 03PRODUCT
One product, multiple entry points

A terminal for navigating the space ecosystem.

Space Intelligence

Follow the signal

Trusted news, research activity, mission developments, university updates, and ecosystem events organized by domain.

Discovery Graph

Explore relationships

Navigate from a technology to relevant papers, institutions, people, projects, companies, and opportunities.

Career Navigation

Find a pathway

Map interests and skills to learning tracks, projects, fellowships, research groups, and roles.

Research Layer

Surface new work

Make university and laboratory research easier to discover across institutional boundaries.

Project Layer

Build proof of work

Connect learning to technical projects and public portfolios rather than stopping at passive content consumption.

AI Interface

Query the ecosystem

Long-term: ask natural-language questions over the graph and receive grounded, source-linked answers.

COSMOHUB // 04WHY NOW
Why this becomes possible now

More space activity creates more information.
AI makes that information navigable.

Signal Growth

More actors

Commercial space, national programs, universities, startups, and open research are generating an expanding volume of public signals.

Data Infrastructure

More machine-readable sources

APIs, RSS, research repositories, public datasets, and structured feeds make aggregation increasingly feasible.

AI + Graphs

Better synthesis

Modern language models and knowledge graphs can help classify, connect, retrieve, and explain complex ecosystem information.

CosmoHub's opportunity is not to create more content. It is to build the navigation layer above existing information.

COSMOHUB // 06BUSINESS MODEL
Monetization hypothesis

Build utility first.
Monetize the intelligence and network layers.

Individual

Premium intelligence

Advanced alerts, saved graphs, personalized tracking, AI research workflows, and professional discovery tools.

Institutional

University and organization profiles

Enhanced discovery, opportunity distribution, ecosystem analytics, and specialized institutional workspaces.

Talent

Matching infrastructure

Long-term revenue from qualified introductions and recruiting workflows once sufficient supply and demand exist.

Data

Intelligence products

Curated datasets, APIs, dashboards, and sector intelligence for organizations that need structured ecosystem visibility.

These are business-model hypotheses to validate; they are not presented as existing revenue streams.

COSMOHUB // 07GO-TO-MARKET
Start narrow, earn expansion

Do not build the whole space economy on day one.

01 // VALIDATE

Space intelligence feed

Aggregate high-quality public signals and test whether a focused audience returns for structured discovery.

02 // CONNECT

Knowledge graph

Link the highest-value entities and topics based on observed user behavior rather than speculative breadth.

03 // PERSONALIZE

Paths and opportunities

Add profiles, saved interests, alerts, project discovery, and opportunity matching.

04 // SCALE

Institutional network

Introduce organization tools, APIs, analytics, and deeper workflows after demonstrating repeated user value.

COSMOHUB // 08MOAT
What could become defensible

The interface is easy to copy.
The connected data layer is not.

Ontology

Domain-specific structure

A continuously refined model of how space technologies, institutions, research, people, projects, and opportunities relate.

Graph

Accumulated relationships

The product becomes more useful as verified entities and connections compound over time.

Behavior

Intent signals

With user consent, discovery and interaction patterns can improve recommendations and reveal unmet ecosystem needs.

The long-term asset is not the dashboard. It is the structured map of the ecosystem beneath it.

COSMOHUB // 1118-MONTH MILESTONES
What the runway must produce

Four milestones before the next round.

01 // PRODUCT

Production MVP

Real source ingestion, structured search, personalization, analytics, and an initial useful knowledge graph.

02 // MARKET

Repeat usage

Target 1,000–3,000 genuinely activated users while measuring cohort retention and recurring product use—not vanity registrations.

03 // INSTITUTIONS

3–5 design partners

Secure credible pilots or design partnerships with universities, research organizations, communities, or space-sector participants.

04 // COMMERCIAL

Willingness to pay

Generate first revenue or sufficiently strong pilot conversion evidence around one clearly defined paid workflow.

These are target milestones for validation, not claims of current traction.

COSMOHUB // 12SEED INFLECTION
What improves for the next financing round

The next round should be triggered by de-risking—not by running out of cash.

Product Risk

From prototype to utility

Evidence that a production product solves a recurring workflow rather than merely demonstrating an attractive concept.

Engagement Risk

From sign-ups to retention

Cohort data showing that a meaningful segment repeatedly returns and receives ongoing value.

Commercial Risk

From interest to payment

Initial revenue, paid pilots, or strong conversion evidence showing that a defined customer values the product economically.

Distribution Risk

From outreach to a channel

Evidence of at least one repeatable path for acquiring users or institutional partners without purely founder-driven outreach.

Data Risk

From feeds to an asset

A reliable ingestion and ontology layer whose structured relationships improve as the product is used.

Seed Story

Scale what works

The seed round then finances expansion of a validated engine instead of continued exploration of an unproven thesis.
